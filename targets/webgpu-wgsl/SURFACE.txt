SCOPE NOTES
- WebGPU API completeness is mechanically checked against the pinned gpuweb/types snapshot by tools/extract_webgpu_types.py.
- WGSL names are pinned to the 25 August 2026 W3C draft; feature/language-extension gates still apply.
- Browser DOM/canvas source-object methods unrelated to WebGPU itself are not duplicated here.
- Native Vulkan/D3D12/Metal/driver opcodes are deliberately not part of the WebGPU target surface.
