# Quad operations

A fragment quad is a small neighboring-lane structure used for derivatives and direct local communication. Apple4 introduces quad-scoped permute capability; Apple6 adds quad ballot; Apple7 adds quad reductions according to Apple's feature tables.

Potential complex-renderer uses: neighboring phase/log-modulus comparisons, local Cauchy-Riemann residuals, winding hints and one-pixel-scale refinement tests.
