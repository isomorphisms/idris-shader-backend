APPLE GPU HARDWARE ISA STATUS

Apple does not publish a stable public machine-opcode ISA/encoding contract for Apple-designed GPUs comparable to Arm A64 or x86-64.

DO NOT invent an opcode list.
DO NOT make reverse-engineered encodings the semantic compiler target.

Public target layers:
- Metal Shading Language (MSL)
- Metal API objects/commands
- Metal GPU-family feature/capability tables

For historical PowerVR-based Apple SoCs, the public app-facing target was OpenGL ES and later Metal; PowerVR microcode/USC details are likewise not a stable Apple application ABI.
